# Likelihood Definition

_Source workbook: URS Entity Info.xlsx_

## Worksheet data

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ​​ | Qualitative​ | Quantitative (Probability)​ |
| Almost Certain​ | The event will occur in most circumstances and is a persistent issue​ | Occur once or more in a year (>75%)​ |
| Likely​ | The event is likely to occur but is not a persistent issue​ | Occur once in 2 years (50%)​ |
| Possible​ | The event may occur occasionally​ | Occur once in 5 years (20%)​ |
| Unlikely​ | The event could occur at some time but the chance is very small​ | Occur once in 20 years (5%)​ |
| Remote​ | The event may occur only in exceptional circumstances​ | Occur once or less in 50 years (≤2%)​ |
